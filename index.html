Directional terms

They come in opposite pairs. Read the pairs, then take the quiz.

PairMeaning
Superior / InferiorToward the head / toward the feet
Anterior / PosteriorFront (ventral) / back (dorsal)
Medial / LateralToward / away from the midline
Proximal / DistalCloser to / farther from the limb's attachment to the trunk
Superficial / DeepNear / far from the body surface
Ipsilateral / ContralateralSame side / opposite side

Intermediate = between a medial and a lateral structure
